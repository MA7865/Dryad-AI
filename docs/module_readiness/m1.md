# M1 - Forest Health

**Status: limited go, with a validity warning.** The layer works end to end. The score level has not been shown to detect reference damage; a modest signal appears in 2022-2024 change (adjusted AUC 0.66, exploratory). The 5-summer trajectory shown on the panel has not been validated (AUC 0.42). Read the output as relative greenness and moisture, not confirmed stress.

## What M1 delivers
For each of the 2,061 patches: a relative Health Index (0-100) with a band (Healthy, Moderate stress, High stress, Post-fire recovery, Not scored), NDVI, NDMI and a 5-summer trajectory compared with similar patches. Latest summer: 2026.

This is a **derived index from observed satellite data**. It is not a trained model and it does not diagnose a cause. The score is relative to unburned forest in this study area.

## Evidence
- **Imagery:** 83 / 85 / 92 / 117 / 113 Sentinel-2 scenes in the 2022-2026 summers. In the 2021-2024 composites, 99.7% of forest pixels had cloud-free data.
- **Forest mask:** about 73% of the area is tree cover. 131 patches (6%) have too little forest to score.
- **Burn context:** about 55% of the area is flagged burned since 2015, mostly the 2020 Creek Fire. 1,132 patches are mostly burned, 817 unburned and 112 partly burned.
- **Bands (2026, unburned scored patches, n = 754):** 90 High stress (12%), 174 Moderate stress (23%), 490 Healthy (65%). The 1,068 scored, mostly burned patches show Post-fire recovery.
- **Trajectory:** slopes for unburned patches centre near zero (median 0.1 points per year; middle half -0.7 to +0.9). 590 of 754 are "Stable vs peers". 813 of 1,132 mostly burned patches are "Recovering".
- **USFS check (exploratory):** the score level does not detect the surveyed damage (AUC 0.18; polygon patches are denser, greener forest). The 2022-2024 change in relative score ranks them better (AUC 0.75; 0.66 after adjusting for starting level, 95% interval 0.60-0.72; 84 polygon patches of 754), but this window was chosen after the first check failed. The panel's 5-summer trend_slope shows no signal (AUC 0.42). See MODEL_EVALUATION.md.

## Risks and limits
- Only 22 USFS damage polygons fall in the study area (2021-2024), mostly light damage and mostly fir engraver. Too few to train or fairly test a classifier.
- No USFS polygon does not mean healthy; it may not have been surveyed.
- The score is a relative ranking of greenness and moisture against unburned forest here. It cannot say whether a low value comes from insects, drought, disease or terrain.
- Burn flag is coarse (MODIS, 500 m). Only 5 summers; summer medians hide changes within a season. Forest mask is from 2021.
- The band cutoffs are a team convention (lowest 10% and 30% of the unburned reference), not validated thresholds.

## How to run
```
pip install -r requirements.txt
python -m modules.forest_health.build_outputs
uvicorn backend.app.main:app --reload
```
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs. To rebuild the feature table, run the scripts in `modules/forest_health/gee/` in the Earth Engine Code Editor (they need the team grid uploaded as an asset) and save the export as `data/processed/m1_features_2022_2026.csv`.
